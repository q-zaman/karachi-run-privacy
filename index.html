Karachi Snatcher Escape - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Karachi Snatcher Escape (package com.karachi.run), published by Meclay Apps ("we", "us").

Summary

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. Your game progress is stored on your phone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect limited device data as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, level, missions, high score, settings such as volume and graphics) is saved in a file on your own phone so the game can continue next time. We do not receive this file. If you uninstall the game, it is deleted.

Internet access

Some features of the game need an internet connection (for example showing ads and online features we may add). We only use the connection for those features.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, to show advertisements. AdMob may collect and use the following data from your device to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and comply with law:

  • Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier) and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language)
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (ads shown, taps, crashes, performance)

We do not receive your personal information from AdMob. How Google uses data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). In regions where the law requires it (such as the EEA and the UK) the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change this choice later.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with Google through AdMob as described above, and with other providers only if we add a new feature, in which case this policy will be updated first.

Permissions

The game uses the internet permission (ads and online features), the network state permission (to check if you are online), the advertising ID permission (AdMob), and the vibration permission (feedback during play, which you can turn off in Settings).

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help.

Your rights and data deletion

Because we do not keep personal data about you on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete; uninstalling the game removes your saved progress from your phone. If you have a question or a request about your data, email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
